# Build My Unicorn (BMU) 2.0 – Product, Commercial & Technical Blueprint

## Core Philosophy: The "Supportive Co-Founder & Blind-Spot Engine"

**"Everyone can have a great idea. It’s everything else around it that makes or breaks the startup."**
First-time founders suffer from "unknown unknowns"—they don't know what they don't know. However, hitting an excited founder with harsh criticism on minute one kills momentum.

Instead, BMU 2.0 follows a **"Capture First, Expand Second"** flow:
1. **Extract & Clarify:** Act as an enthusiastic, structured sounding board to get the messy idea out of the founder's head and onto paper cleanly.
2. **"Things to Consider" (The Blind-Spot Review):** Once the vision is captured at the end of a module, gently surface the hidden operational realities, industry traps, and questions they haven't thought about yet—framed as constructive prompts rather than roadblocks.

---

## Section 0: 4-Layer Built-In Support Architecture (Zero-Video, Low Overhead)
1. **Inline Micro-Coaching ("Why Ask This?" + "Show Me an Example"):** Every question has a 1-click toggle explaining why investors/grant boards care, plus 2–3 AI-generated sample answers tailored to the founder's specific industry.
2. **Context-Aware AI Sidekick ("Ask Unicorn"):** A slide-out helper aware of the active Plan and Module Step, featuring an *"I'm Stuck — Interview Me"* button that breaks tough inputs into two simple chat questions.
3. **Momentum Protection ("Park It" + Industry Benchmarks):** Founders can click *"I don't know yet — Park in Open Questions"* or *"Insert Industry Benchmark Estimate"* on quantitative steps so they never stall out.
4. **White-Label Mentor Escalation (Phase 2 Ready):** Allows cohort founders to flag a specific module for their Accelerator/LEO mentor to review.

## Section 1: The 9 Core Founder Modules (Modules A–I)

### Module A: Ideation, Founder DNA & Vision Extraction (The "Free Hook" Experience)
*Because Module A is the free experience before the paywall, it cannot feel like a basic 4-box form. It must give the founder goosebumps—helping them understand **themselves** and their **business** so deeply in 15 minutes that upgrading feels like a no-brainer.*

* **Step 1: The "Founder North Star & Motivation" Check ("Why Are You Doing This?")**
  * **Your Personal Driver:** Why start this now? *(e.g., "Solve a problem I lived through personally", "Escape the 9-to-5 & own my time", "Build generational wealth / big exit", "Create local impact / jobs").*
  * **The "Success Definition" (3 Years From Now):**
    * **Path A: The "Cashflow & Freedom" Engine (Bootstrapped / Grant-Backed):** Profitable by Month 9, 100% owned by you, small nimble team, pays you a great salary.
    * **Path B: The "High-Growth Scale / Unicorn" Track (Investor-Backed):** Fast international expansion, raising Seed/Series A, aiming for a €10M–€100M+ exit.
  * **Your Non-Negotiables & Guardrails:** Available hours/week, max personal savings willing to risk, and technical comfort level.
* **Step 2: Frictionless Brain Dump ("Tell the Story of Your Idea")**
  * Voice dictation (microphone) or free-text input where the founder explains the lightbulb moment and how the idea works in their own words.
* **Step 3: Instant 1-Page Concept Canvas + "Name & Tagline Polisher"**
  * AI mirrors their messy brain dump back as a crisp **1-Page Concept Canvas** (*The Problem, The Target Customer, The Core Solution, The "Why Now" Edge*) AND generates **3 punchy 1-Sentence Elevator Pitches**.
* **Step 4: Visual "Customer Journey Storyboard" ("Before vs. After Your Startup")**
  * Generates a visual 3-step **"Old Painful Way"** vs. **"New Way With Your Product"** flow so they see their product through a real human's eyes.
* **Step 5: Guided Business Model Archetype & Localization Setup**
  * Sets HQ Country (`IE`, `GB`, `US`, `AU`, `CA`, `EU`), Base Currency, Target Markets, and Industry.
* **Step 6: The "Idea Stress-Test & Fit Scorecard" (Founder-Market Fit)**
  * Checks if the **Founder's Motivation & Guardrails (Step 1)** match the **Business Idea (Steps 2–5)** and suggests how to reshape the idea if there is a mismatch.
* **Step 7: "Things to Consider" Blind-Spot Preview + The "Unicorn Teaser Blueprint" (The Paywall Bridge)**
  * Surfaces 3–5 constructive blind spots and displays a tailored preview of the Grants, Hosted Customer Survey Page, Product Build Blueprint, and Co-Founder Interview waiting behind the Launch Pass.
* **Step 8 (Optional Power Tool): Multi-Plan Idea Comparator**
  * Side-by-side scoring matrix for founders comparing 2+ ideas against their Step 1 Personal Motivations.

### Module B: Market Research & Competitive Intelligence
* **Step 1: Beachhead Customer Selector:** Narrows down from "everyone" to the #1 most urgent buyer persona (who has the hair-on-fire problem today, where they hang out online/offline, and what triggers them to buy).
* **Step 2: AI Market & Competitor Scout:** Identifies direct competitors, indirect alternatives, AND the "Status Quo" workaround (spreadsheets, WhatsApp, manual agencies) that the startup is actually competing against.
* **Step 3: Plain-English Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM):** Bottom-up market sizing calculator using real customer counts and pricing rather than top-down consulting jargon.
* **Step 4: Competitive Wedge & Defensibility Matrix:** Maps how the startup wins on speed, price, niche focus, or convenience without triggering a price war.
* **Step 5: "Market Things to Consider":** Flags switching friction, regulatory barriers, and market concentration risks.

### Module C: Customer Surveys, Feedback & Early Waitlist (Standalone Validation Engine)
* **Why This Is a Flagship Selling Point:** Most founders either skip customer research completely or send out biased Google Forms asking *"Would you use my cool idea?"* (where friends lie and say yes). BMU 2.0 includes a built-in **Hosted Survey, Feedback & Early Waitlist Engine** (`buildmyunicorn.com/v/[slug]`) that turns real customer feedback directly into chart-ready proof for grant boards and investors.

* **Step 1: The AI "Mom Test" Survey Architect (Bias-Free Question Generator)**
  * Automatically drafts 5–8 high-converting survey questions based on the founder's Module A Concept and Module B Beachhead Persona.
  * **Built-In "Mom Test" Bias Detector:** If a founder types a leading question like *"Would you pay €20 for our awesome app?"*, the AI flags it and rewrites it into a behavioral truth question: *"How much did you spend last month trying to solve [Problem], and what was the most frustrating part?"*

* **Step 2: Hosted Public Survey & Early-Access Waitlist Page (`buildmyunicorn.com/v/[slug]`)**
  * One-click publishing of a clean, mobile-optimized, distraction-free public survey page hosted directly on BMU (no Typeform or SurveyMonkey subscription needed).
  * **Optional "Early Bird Waitlist" Lead Capture:** At the end of the survey, respondents can opt in (*"Want early access or a 30% founder discount when we launch? Drop your email"*), building the founder's very first warm customer list automatically!

* **Step 3: The "Where to Get 25 Responses in 48 Hours" Outreach Kit**
  * First-time founders build a survey and then freeze because they don't know who to send it to.
  * Generates tailored, non-spammy outreach templates to share the survey link across **LinkedIn DMs, Reddit/Facebook Groups, WhatsApp communities, and cold email**—plus a printable **QR Code flyer** for founders validating local/physical businesses in person.

* **Step 4: 1-on-1 Customer Interview Script & Call Notes Logger**
  * For B2B or high-ticket startups where 1-on-1 conversations matter as much as surveys, provides a structured **15-Minute Discovery Call Script** and a quick logger where founders can paste raw call notes or voice transcripts.

* **Step 5: Live Response Dashboard, AI Sentiment Synthesis & "Pivot or Proceed" Scorecard**
  * Real-time analytics showing response counts, pain-point severity scores, price-sensitivity curves, and captured waitlist leads.
  * **AI Feedback Synthesizer:** Once responses roll in, Gemini analyzes every open-text answer to highlight:
    1. *Top 3 Validated Pain Points (in the customer's exact words—ready to use as marketing copy in Module G).*
    2. *Pricing Sweet Spot & Willingness to Pay (syncs to Module F).*
    3. *Feature Requests vs. Fluff (syncs to Module D).*
  * **Auto-Injected Proof into Module I (Business Plan & Grant Editor):** Automatically generates a *"Primary Market Validation Evidence"* chart block for LEO/Enterprise Ireland grant applications, proving the founder talked to real customers!

* **Step 6: "Survey & Validation Things to Consider" (The Blind-Spot Review)**
  * Flags "False Positive" traps (e.g., surveying friends and family instead of real buyers, tiny sample sizes, or high interest with zero willingness to pay).

### Module D: "What Are We Actually Building?" (Product Vehicle, Build-vs-Buy & Delivery Architecture)
* **The Core Blind Spot:** First-time founders immediately think *"I need to hire developers for €40k to build a custom app from scratch"* OR *"I need to build a custom website"*—when in reality, they could launch in 2 weeks using an existing marketplace (Amazon/Etsy), a Shopify store, a No-Code/White-Label stack, or a "Wizard of Oz" manual service. Conversely, founders selling on Amazon often forget they don't own the customer relationship and lose 15–35% in FBA fees.

* **Step 1: The "Product Vehicle" Selector ("What Is the Actual Thing the Customer Buys or Uses?")**
  * Helps the founder define the exact delivery vehicle across 4 clear categories (or a hybrid):
    1. **Physical Product / Consumer Goods:** *(e.g., Food & Beverage, Hardware/IoT, Apparel, Cosmetics, Print-on-Demand, White-Label vs. Custom Manufactured).*
    2. **Software / Digital Product:** *(e.g., B2B Web SaaS, Mobile App iOS/Android, Browser Extension, AI Wrapper/Agent, Two-Sided Digital Marketplace).*
    3. **Service / Expertise / Agency:** *(e.g., Productized Service, Consulting/Coaching, Local Brick-and-Mortar Service, Staffing/Recruiting).*
    4. **Content / Community / Education:** *(e.g., Paid Community, Cohort Course, B2B Data/Research Subscription).*

* **Step 2: The "Where Does It Live?" Channel & Platform Decision Matrix (Own vs. Rent the Shelf)**
  * **For Physical / E-Commerce Products — *"Marketplace vs. Own Storefront"*:**
    * **Option A: Third-Party Marketplaces (Amazon FBA/FBM, Etsy, TikTok Shop, eBay, Retail Stockists):**
      * *Pros:* Built-in buyer traffic on Day 1, high trust, fulfillment handled (FBA).
      * *The Blind Spot:* Amazon takes 15–35% fees, holds your payout reserves for weeks, owns the customer email (you can't remarket to them easily), and can clone your best-selling product.
    * **Option B: Your Own D2C Store (Shopify, WooCommerce):**
      * *Pros:* You own 100% of the brand, customer emails, repeat orders, and margins.
      * *The Blind Spot:* Zero traffic on Day 1—every single visitor must be won through ads, SEO, or social hustle.
    * **Option C: The "Trojan Horse" Hybrid:** Launch on Amazon/Marketplaces for instant cashflow + insert QR warranty/reorder cards inside the packaging to pull repeat buyers onto your own Shopify store.
  * **For Software / Tech Products — *"Web App vs. Native Mobile App vs. Plugin"*:**
    * Challenges the #1 founder mistake: *"Do you actually need an iOS/Android App Store app on Day 1, or should this be a mobile-friendly Web App?"* (Explaining that Apple/Google take 15–30% commission, require weeks of review for every bug fix, and that 80% of customers won't download a brand-new app just to try a service).

* **Step 3: The "Build vs. Buy vs. Stitch" Architecture Recommender (How to Build Version 1 for 10% of the Cost)**
  * Based on their budget and technical skills from Module A, the AI maps out **3 Realistic Build Tiers** for their exact idea:
    * **Tier 1: The "Weekend Concierge / No-Code Stitch" (€0–€500, 1–2 Weeks):** How to fake the backend manually using off-the-shelf tools (e.g., Typeform + Stripe Payment Links + WhatsApp/Airtable/Zapier, or white-labeling an existing product) to get the first 10 paying customers *before* writing a line of code or ordering 5,000 factory units.
    * **Tier 2: The "Low-Code / Shopify / White-Label MVP" (€500–€5,000, 4–6 Weeks):** Using Shopify + apps, Bubble/FlutterFlow, or small-batch local production to validate repeat purchases.
    * **Tier 3: The "Custom Build from Scratch / Custom Manufacturing" (€15,000–€100,000+, 3–6 Months):** Custom code (Next.js/Mobile) or custom factory tooling/molds—unlocking true defensibility and IP once validated.
  * **Founder Action:** The founder selects which Build Tier they are committing to for **Day 1 (Launch)** vs. **Month 12 (Scale)**.

* **Step 4: Product Scope & The Ruthless "Version 1" Feature/SKU Filter**
  * Pulls customer feature requests directly from **Module C (Surveys & Feedback)** and separates them into:
    * **For Software:** **"Must-Have for Day 1"** (max 3 core features that solve the main problem) vs. **"Phase 2 Wishlist"**.
    * **For Physical Products:** **"Hero SKU"** (launching with 1 flagship product/flavor/size to keep minimum order quantities and packaging costs low) vs. **"SKU Creep"**.

* **Step 5: Sourcing, Prototyping & Technical Spec Generator**
  * **For Software:** Generates a clean **1-Page Developer / No-Code Build Spec** (User Roles, Core Database Entities, Third-Party APIs needed, and Security/GDPR requirements) so they never get overcharged by an agency.
  * **For Physical Products:** Generates a **Supplier & Packaging Spec Sheet** (Target landed unit cost, MOQ questions to ask factories on Alibaba/local manufacturers, sampling checklist, and barcode/labeling compliance).

* **Step 6: "Supply Chain, Tech Stack & Build Things to Consider" (The Blind-Spot Review)**
  * **Physical Product Traps Flagged:** Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs), lead times from China vs. local suppliers, customs/import duties, barcode (GS1) registration, product liability insurance, and shelf-life/returns handling.
  * **Software Build Traps Flagged:** Hiring a cheap agency without owning the GitHub repo from Day 1, building native iOS/Android simultaneously instead of cross-platform or web, and ongoing monthly maintenance/hosting costs.
  * **Direct Data Sync to Modules E, F & H:** Automatically pushes their chosen Build Cost, Lead Times, Platform Fees (e.g., Amazon 15% referral fee or Shopify 2.9% fee), and Inventory/Hosting costs straight into Operations (Module E), Unit Economics (Module F), and the Financial Builder (Module H)!

## Section 6: Brainstorming New High-Impact Features (Easy, Medium & Hard)

### ⭐ FEATURE SPOTLIGHT: The "Reverse-Engineered Launch Calendar" (Backwards Countdown Engine)
* **Difficulty:** 🟡 **Medium (3–4 Days)**
* **The Core Idea:** Founders almost always pick a launch date out of thin air (*"We're launching on February 28th!"*), but they plan **forwards** instead of **backwards**. Because they don't know the hidden lead times for legal, banking, grant approvals, manufacturing/app store reviews, or warm-up marketing, they miss their launch date by 4 months.
* **How It Works Step-by-Step:**
  1. **Pick the Target Launch Date ("T-Zero"):** The founder enters their goal date (e.g., **Feb 28**) and how many hours per week the team actually has available (pulled from their Module E Co-Founder Interview).
  2. **Automatic Backwards Dependency Mapping:** Counts **backwards** from Feb 28 across 5 tracks (Product/Manufacturing/App Store buffers from Module D, Marketing waitlist warm-up from Module G, Legal/Banking lead times from Module H, Grant committee approval lead times, and Open Questions deadlines).
  3. **The "Impossible Timeline" Collision Alert:** Flags if Step 1 needed to start in the past and offers 1-click fixes (push date, switch from Tier 3 Custom Build to Tier 1/2 MVP in Module D, or decouple bottlenecks).
